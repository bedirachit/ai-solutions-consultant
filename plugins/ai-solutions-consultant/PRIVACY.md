# Privacy

The AI Solutions Consultant plugin contains only instructions (skills and agent prompts), reference material and one local helper script. It does not collect, store or transmit any data of its own and has no telemetry.

When you use it, your content is processed by Claude under your Anthropic account terms, and by any connectors you choose to connect (for example draw.io, document or wiki tools), each under its own privacy policy:

- draw.io: https://www.drawio.com/privacy

The "Open in draw.io" link encodes the diagram inside the link itself; anyone you share the link with can open the diagram. Do not share links to diagrams that contain confidential information.
